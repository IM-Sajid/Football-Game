## 🎮 About the Game

**2 Player Football Game** is a local multiplayer game where two players go head-to-head on the same device. Each player controls a bar (goalkeeper) to deflect the ball and protect their goalpost. Every time a player successfully hits the ball, it speeds up slightly — making each rally more intense and challenging than the last!

Think of it as **Pong meets Football** — simple to learn, impossible to put down. ⚡

---

## 🎯 How to Play

| | Details |
|---|---|
| **Players** | 2 (Local Multiplayer) |
| **Controls** | Each player drags their bar up and down on their side of the screen |
| **Objective** | Deflect the ball to prevent it from crossing your goalpost |
| **Scoring** | A goal is scored when the ball passes the opponent's bar |
| **Twist** | The ball gets **slightly faster** with every hit! 🔥 |
